# CL-214: Put Linear delivery proof in checks, not PR prose

## Goal

A Linear-routed PR is gated by its required checks and `agent-review`. Proof
lives in those checks and their CI artifacts, not in a Bugbot wait or screenshots
attached to Linear.

## Context

`deliver-linear` required Bugbot to have reviewed the head, required screenshots
or recordings to be uploaded to Linear, and said that green CI never substitutes
for Bugbot. Research on this template found that such evidence gates in PR prose
slow repositories. The gate that Auto-merge reads is `agent-review`.

The change removes a review gate, so agent review reports an `owner` finding.
That is the intended trade. The owner decides whether to keep it.

## Steps

1. `deliver-linear/SKILL.md`: replace "Bugbot and evidence gates" with a short
   "Review and proof" section. The checks and `agent-review` are the gate. Bugbot
   is read like any review comment, never awaited.
2. Remove the upload of screenshots and recordings to Linear. One Linear comment
   links the PR.
3. Update the `description:` frontmatter to drop "retained verification evidence
   and Bugbot review".

## Files

- `.agents/skills/deliver-linear/SKILL.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- The skill names no Bugbot wait and no screenshot upload as a requirement.
- The PR body names the tested SHA and the preview URL when there is one.
- A visible change with no covering journey gets a journey, not a screenshot.

## Verify

`mise exec -- bun run validate`, and a read of the changed skill against
`.agents/skills/deliver-linear/SKILL.md` for leftover Bugbot or screenshot rules.
