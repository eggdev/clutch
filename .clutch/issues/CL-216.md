# CL-216: Step auto-merge down when reverts pass 2%

## Goal

Auto-merge steps itself down when reverts pass 2% of merged PRs in 30 days, as decision 0002 says. Gardening PRs may also auto-merge at risk `medium`.

## Context

- Decision 0002 says to tighten when reverts pass 2% in a 30-day window. Until now only the weekly digest showed the rate (`apps/cli/src/metrics/report.ts`), and nothing acted on it.
- Gardening PRs auto-merged only at tier `low`, so most of their output waited for a person.

## Steps

1. In `tools/github/auto-merge-rules.ts`, add `brake`, `brakedStep` and `brakeReason`.
2. In `tools/github/auto-merge-brake.ts`, count the PRs merged in the window with one `gh pr list` call, and judge the PR at the braked step. A failed count keeps the configured step and logs a `::warning::`.
3. In `tools/github/auto-merge.ts`, use the braked step for the blockers, the handoff, the enabled marker and the dry-run calibration. Allow a gardening PR at tier `medium`.
4. Document the brake in `pull-requests.md#auto-merge`, and in the "Revisit if" of decision 0002.

## Files

- `tools/github/auto-merge.ts`, `tools/github/auto-merge-brake.ts`, `tools/github/auto-merge-rules.ts`
- `tools/github/auto-merge-judge.test.ts`, `tools/github/auto-merge-fake.ts`
- `.agents/skills/deliver/references/pull-requests.md`, `docs/contributing/decisions/0002-merge-most-prs-with-no-human.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- At 3 reverts in 40 merges, a `medium` agent PR is judged at `low`. At 1 in 60, or 3 in 10, it is not braked.
- A failed count does not brake the run.
- A medium gardening PR merges at the step `bots`.

## Verify

`mise exec -- bun test tools/github/auto-merge` and `mise exec -- bun run validate`.
