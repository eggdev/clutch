# CL-590: A connection gets a live reader only once the reader knows its start

## Goal

A connection that subscribes while the live reader of its organization is being made gets every event: each one comes in its backlog or in its live stream, with no hole.

## Context

The review of 2026-11-20 found this by reading `packages/sync/service/live.ts` and `packages/sync/service/feed.ts`.

The first connection of an organization makes its reader and puts the reader in the map before the reader reads the head of the log. A second connection subscribes at once, reads its own head and its backlog. When an event E commits after that backlog read and before the head read of the reader, the reader starts after E. Neither the backlog nor the live stream sends E to the second connection, and the timed read starts from the same point. The client stays wrong until it connects again.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-590-live-reader-start` from `origin/main`.
2. Write the test first. A Drizzle query logger in the test holds the first head read of the reader, so the race happens on each run.
3. Keep the start position of each reader in a `Deferred`. A subscriber waits for it before it gets the subscription, so its own head read, and so its backlog, reach at least that position. The reader reads its head in a fiber of its own scope, so an interrupted first subscriber leaves no reader that never starts.

## Acceptance criteria

- A second connection that subscribes while the reader reads its head gets each event that commits in that time.
- A reader whose head read fails leaves the map, and each connection that waited for it fails, so its client connects again.
- Each existing test of the live reader passes unchanged.
