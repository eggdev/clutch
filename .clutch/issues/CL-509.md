# CL-509: Registry v2: add the levers the research ranks highest and mark trigger-based capabilities

## Goal

The capability registry measures the levers that predict throughput, and it stops nagging about capabilities a project needs only at scale.

## Context

In the 2026-10-30 research over 58 repositories (see `.clutch/PLAN.md`), the 36 seed capabilities came from a ranking by raw volume. Re-scored by what lets a repository ship more than its headcount predicts, labels, revert, a PR babysitter, agent-file budgets, contract checks, deprecation policy and flow metrics rank high, and the merge queue and PR size limits show no link.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-509-registry-v2`.
2. Add entries: `pr_labels`, `revert_bot`, `pr_babysitter`, `agents_md_chain_budget`, `contract_check`, `deprecation_policy`. `factory_observability` detects `clutch metrics`.
3. Add a `trigger` field: a capability with a trigger is `n/a` until its condition holds, such as "2 or more engineers" or "CI over 10 minutes". Use it for `merge_queue`, `test_impact`, `sensitive_path_approval` and dense CODEOWNERS.
4. Update `docs/contributing/capabilities/` with one page per new entry.

## Files

- `docs/contributing/capabilities/registry.yaml` and pages
- `apps/cli/src/doctor/registry.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- `doctor` reports the new entries on Clutch.
- A trigger-based capability with its trigger unmet is `n/a`, not a gap.
