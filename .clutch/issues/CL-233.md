# CL-233: Keep revert drills out of the brake and the revert metric

## Goal

A revert rehearsal does not count as a real revert, so it cannot brake auto-merge or fail the revert rate.

## Context

- On Neighborhood, a deliberate revert drill (#48 merged, then reverted by #49) counted as a real revert: 1 of 48 merged PRs, 2.08%, over the 2% brake. The brake stepped auto-merge from `medium` to `low`.
- The rollback docs ask for a rehearsal each quarter, so every drill would brake.

## Steps

1. A `drill` label, created with `revert` by the Labels workflow, on the merged PR to rehearse.
2. The Revert workflow labels the revert PR `drill` too, and files no `risk-tighten` issue.
3. The brake does not count a merged `revert/` PR labelled `drill`. It still counts in `merged`.
4. `clutch metrics` excludes drill reverts from the reverts, shows the drills apart, and adds `reverts.drills` to the JSON.
5. Docs: the Rehearsal section of rollback.md and the labels table in review.md.

## Acceptance criteria

- A revert of a PR labelled `drill` carries `drill` and files no risk-tighten issue. Its body says it is a rehearsal.
- 1 drill in 48 merged PRs does not brake. 1 real revert in 48 does.
- Metrics count a drill apart from reverts, on the PRs and on main, and print it on the Reverts row.
- Auto-revert is unchanged.
- The trade-off is in the PR body: anyone who can label a PR can mark a real revert as a drill.
