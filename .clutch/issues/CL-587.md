# CL-587: Save the actor of each waiting transaction and end the session in every tab

## Goal

A waiting transaction of one person is never sent as another person, also when two tabs share the device and one of them ends the session.

## Context

The review of 2026-11-20 found this, and confirmed it with a probe. User U1 has tabs A (the writer) and B. U1 signs out in tab A while offline, and A clears the local database. B still runs U1's pool. It saves a new waiting transaction, and then the tab closes before it connects. User U2 signs in on tab A, and A writes a position row with actor U2. On A's next start, the transaction of U1 loads under actor U2, passes the actor check of CL-919, and is sent as U2.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-587-waiting-actor`, stacked on CL-586.
2. Move the tables that screens see, their ID lists and their watchers into `tables.ts`, and the helpers of the waiting queue beside the outcomes of a push, so `pool.ts` has room under its 400 lines.
3. Write the failing tests first: a row of another actor is dropped at the load and at `confirmActor`, a row with no actor takes the confirmed actor, a new row holds the actor, and a session end in one tab stops the saves of another.
4. Each saved waiting transaction holds the actor that ran it, once the pool knows its actor. A row of another actor is dropped, never sent, and reported in one sentence.
5. After the review of the branch: `clear` leaves the mark of a session end, and the pool drops each waiting row with no actor while it is there. The first save of the next actor removes the mark, and each row with no actor that the save does not write. A row that takes the confirmed actor is saved again with it.
6. `clear` tells each other tab of the database through a `BroadcastChannel` on the web. `followSessionEnd` stops the engine and saves of a tab that hears it, and starts the app again.
7. Update `local-first-sync.md`, `client.md` and `data-storage.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- The probe case: a row of U1 under a position of U2 is not in the queue, is not sent, is reported once, and leaves the local database.
- A row with no actor takes the actor that the server confirms next.
- The pool sends no actor to the server: the protocol does not change.
- A session end in one tab stops the engine and saves of each other tab before it writes anything back, and that tab starts again.
- Each adapter keeps the actor of a waiting row.
- After a session end, a waiting row with no actor is never sent as the next person: the clear leaves a mark, the pool drops such rows while the mark is there, and the first save of the next actor removes the mark with each row with no actor that it does not write. A copy with no mark and no actor keeps its rows with no actor.
- A pool that loads a saved actor gives it to each new row, also offline.
