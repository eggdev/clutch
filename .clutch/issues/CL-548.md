# CL-548: Limit the time rule in the checks page to the time of a run

## Goal

The checks page says that the time of a check or a CI run does not block, and it does not forbid a performance budget of the product.

## Context

CL-535 added the section "Time is reported, and a count blocks" to `docs/contributing/setup/checks.md`. Its first paragraph said: "Do not add a check that blocks on time." That is too wide. The section "Performance budgets" on the same page tells a performance check to take three samples and compare the best one with a budget. `docs/contributing/architecture/client-performance.md` sets a 50 ms budget for input to next paint. CL-535 merged when its checks passed, before this correction reached its branch.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-548-time-rule-scope`.
2. Say in the first paragraph that the rule is for the time that a check or a CI run takes on a runner.
3. Say that a performance budget of the product is another case, and link the section "Performance budgets".

## Files

- `docs/contributing/setup/checks.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- The section limits its rule to the time of a run.
- The section links the performance budgets of the product as another case.
