# CL-527: Decide the cap on blocking checks and the not-doing list

## Goal

The owner decides whether Clutch caps its blocking checks and keeps a binding not-doing list, as Exact does.

## Context

Exact holds itself to six blocking checks, and a seventh needs one deleted in the same change. Its `rules/DEFERRED.md` is binding, and each addition names what comes off. Clutch blocks on 43 targets: 7 in `tools/validate.ts` and 36 under `checks` in `package.json`. About 8 hold prose, 13 hold process, 4 hold security and 10 hold the contract and the shape of the repository. Removing a check weakens a guardrail, so this is the owner's decision.

The owner decided on 2026-11-10: the checks stay, because the project still needs some of them. One check changes to Exact's form: `check-bundle` counts the modules that load before the first paint, and its byte budget is gone. No cap on the number of checks is set.

## Steps

1. Decide whether a cap comes later, and whether the prose checks move to a lane that does not block.
2. Decide whether P4's exit metric in `.clutch/PLAN.md` stays.
3. Write the decision record, and the not-doing list if there is one.

## Files

- `docs/contributing/decisions/`
- `.clutch/PLAN.md`
- `package.json`, `tools/validate.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- A decision record states the cap and the list, or says why Clutch has neither.
