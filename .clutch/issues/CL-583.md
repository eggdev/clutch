# CL-583: Load a group from the last state of its records

## Goal

The first load of a client and the load of a group cost what the records cost, not what their history costs. This is the first part of CL-917. CL-917 keeps the trim of the log.

## Context

A council review on 2026-11-20 compared the sync engine with a study of Linear's client. `backlog` and `backfill` in `packages/sync/service/event-log.ts` read each past event of a group, so a record that changed 1,000 times travels 1,000 times to each new device. CL-530 added `sync_records`, which holds the last change of each record. A delta that carries the last change of each record is a snapshot, and the pool already keeps the change with the higher sync ID.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-583-snapshot-loads`.
2. Read a snapshot of a group from `sync_records`, in pages by the record key. Leave deleted records out.
3. Send it for `Load`, for an eager group that a client did not name, and for a `Pull` at sync ID 0. Keep the wire shape.
4. After the snapshot of a `Pull` at sync ID 0, send an empty delta at the head, so the client saves that sync ID.
5. Test a record that changes, one that is deleted and one that enters the group between two pages.

## Files

- `packages/sync/repo/records.ts`, `packages/sync/service/event-log.ts`, `packages/sync/service/feed.ts`
- `packages/sync/runtime/tools.server.ts`, `docs/contributing/architecture/local-first-sync.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- A group load sends each live record once, whatever its history.
- A client that does not ask for pages still gets one message, and no message is new to an older build.
- A record that changes, leaves or enters a group while its pages go out ends right in the pool.
