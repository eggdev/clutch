# CL-702: Port the remaining legacy pr.yml jobs into ci.yml on epic/refactor

## Goal

- `check-lint-suppressions.cjs` (merge-base count)
- `check-anti-patterns.sh`
- the vitest shards
- the SHA-pinned actions

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-702-ci-cutover` from `epic/refactor`.

3. Delete `pr.yml` only when nothing is left. Changing the required checks to match is part of CL-705.

## Acceptance criteria

- `pr.yml` is gone, and every job is accounted for in the PR body.
- `ci.yml` is green.
