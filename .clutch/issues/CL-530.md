# CL-530: Tell the clients of a group when a record leaves it

## Goal

A client that loaded a group stops holding a record once the record is no longer in that group.

## Context

The groups of a record are chosen at each write. A changed group field, or a `when` or `unless` condition that flips, moves the record to other groups. The event log sends the change only to the groups that the record names after the write, so a client of the old group keeps its copy until it loads the group again. A review of the contract work found this. The template has no command that moves a record between groups, so no screen shows it yet. A project with conditional groups meets it at once.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-530-leave-group`.
2. Write the failing tests first: in `packages/sync/runtime/pool-conformance.test.ts` and `packages/sync/runtime/tools.server.test.ts`, a record whose group field changes is gone for a client of the old group.
3. Carry the groups that the record had before the write on its change, and send a delete of the record to each group that it left.
4. Decide what a record with no group after a write means: a delete for every client, or a stopped commit as today.

## Files

- `packages/sync/contract/change.ts`, `packages/sync/service/event-log.ts`, `packages/sync/repo/events.ts`
- `packages/sync/runtime/pool.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- A client of the old group does not hold the record after the delta of the move.
- A read tool of the old group does not list it.
