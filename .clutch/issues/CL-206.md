# CL-206: Make tests of gate code low risk

## Goal

A PR that changes only tests, including tests of the automation and its
gates, is `risk/low`, so auto-merge at the step `low` can take it.

## Context

Brendan measures the P2 goal (half of PRs merge with no person) on Clutch
itself (T-005). Since the step `low` went live in v0.32.0, no PR has merged
without a person. Of the 23 PRs merged from #100 to #131, 5 were `low` and 18
were `one-way-door`. The globs that set them:

- `.agents/skills/**` (agent steering): 9 PRs.
- `clutch.yaml` and `.clutch/doctor-baseline.json`: 8 PRs.
- `tools/github/**`: 6 PRs. `.github/workflows/**`: 5.
- `clutch.files.yaml`: 4. `tools/check-*.ts` and `tools/validate.ts`: 3.
- A test file under a guardrail glob: 6 PRs, and the only cause in #102.

Steering, `clutch.yaml` and the gates stay a one-way door (PLAN.md decision
6). A test of a gate is not the gate: changing it changes no check.
`check-test-integrity` fails a change that deletes a test or lowers its test
or assertion count, and its allow list stays a one-way door.

## Steps

1. Give the first one-way-door rule in `.clutch/risk.yaml` an `except` for
   `**/*.test.ts` and `**/*.test.tsx`. Move steering, auth and migrations to a
   second rule, so their tests stay a one-way door.
2. Let `tools/codeowners.ts` write that `except` as CODEOWNERS lines with no
   owner, before the later rules' lines. Only the first one-way-door rule may
   have `except`.
3. Regenerate `.github/CODEOWNERS`. Update the risk and guardrail docs.

4. Make decision records (`docs/contributing/decisions/**`) and principles
   (`docs/principles/**`) one-way-door as steering: #132 merged decision
   record 0001 as low risk, with no review. Add `packages/journeys/**`, which
   holds the accessibility check from #136, to the guardrail list.

## Files

- `.clutch/risk.yaml`, `tools/codeowners.ts`, `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- `tools/codeowners.test.ts`, `apps/cli/src/risk-command.test.ts`
- `apps/cli/README.md`, `docs/contributing/harness-engineering.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- `tools/github/auto-merge.test.ts` alone is `low`. `tools/github/auto-merge.ts`
  and `packages/access/**/*.test.ts` stay `one-way-door`.
- CODEOWNERS names no owner for a test file, except under steering, auth and
  migration paths.
- A decision record, a principle and `packages/journeys/testing/a11y.ts` are
  `one-way-door`.
- Of the 23 PRs merged from #100 to #131, 6 are `low` (before: 5).

## Verify

`mise exec -- bun run validate`, and `clutch risk` on the PR itself.
