# CL-463: Ask the owner only for a change that cannot be paid back

## Goal

The owner signs off a major change. A pull request waits for him by a path rule only where a mistake cannot be paid back later. Each other change merges on proof.

## Context

- The owner said on 2026-11-12 that the project asks for him too often. He wants about 5 in 100 pull requests, and he would pay a problem back after he finds it. He stays in the loop for the story, the prototype, the design and the sign-off of a major change.
- In the last 100 merged PRs, 34 waited for him by a path rule: 28 for the code and the rules of the merge gate, 10 for decision records and policy docs, and 1 for auth and access. None was a major change.
- The council review of 2026-11-12 asked for more holds. Its rule would hold 90 in 100. The owner chose the other direction.
- 15 of the 100 added a write permission, a secret or a privileged trigger to a workflow. A leaked credential cannot be paid back, so this issue keeps that hold. The owner may drop it.
- CL-464 holds a contract break, which is a major change of the contracts.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-463-owner-signs-majors`.
2. In `.clutch/risk.yaml`, keep the tier `one-way-door` for the credential and secret-scanning files, for auth, access and identity, for migrations, and for a workflow line that adds a grant. Remove the rules for the merge gate, for decision records and policy docs, and for the merge lines of `clutch.yaml`.
3. Run `mise exec -- bun tools/codeowners.ts --write`.
4. Write the failing tests first, then hold a major change in the merge judge: a title with `!` before the colon, or a changeset in the diff that names a major bump. The owner's approval lifts it.
5. Write decision record 0005, and mark 0002 as amended by it.
6. Move each doc that lists the hard core: `docs/contributing/review.md`, `docs/contributing/harness-engineering.md`, `docs/principles/proof-gates-code.md` and `.agents/skills/deliver/references/pull-requests.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `clutch risk` gives no `one-way-door` for a change to `.clutch/risk.yaml`, to `tools/github/auto-merge-rules.ts`, or to a page under `docs/principles/`.
- It gives `one-way-door` for `.mcp.json`, for a file under `packages/access/`, for a migration, and for a workflow line that adds `contents: write`. A workflow line `contents: read` is not held.
- A PR with a `!` title or a major changeset waits for the owner, and his approval lifts the hold.
- On the last 100 merged PRs, the new rule holds 16.
