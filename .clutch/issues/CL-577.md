# CL-577: Send a group backfill in pages, and limit the size of one push

## Goal

No message of the sync socket grows without a limit.

## Context

A council review on 2026-11-20 found two messages with no limit.

- `eventsUpTo` in `packages/sync/repo/events.ts` reads the whole history of a group, and `onLoad` in `packages/sync/service/feed.ts` sends it as one `Groups` message. The backlog of a `Pull` already goes in pages of 1,000 events.
- `push` in `packages/sync/runtime/transport.ts` sends every unsent transaction in one `Push`. A device that was offline for a long time sends one large message. Linear sends at most 40 transactions in one request.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-577-bounded-messages`.
2. Write the failing tests first, in `packages/sync/service/feed.test.ts` and `packages/sync/runtime/transport.test.ts`.
3. Send the backfill of a group in pages. The gate resumes after the last page.
4. Send at most a fixed number of transactions in one `Push`, in their order.

## Files

- `packages/sync/repo/events.ts`, `packages/sync/service/event-log.ts`, `packages/sync/service/feed.ts`
- `packages/sync/runtime/transport.ts`, `packages/sync/runtime/pool.ts`
- `packages/sync/types/protocol.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- A backfill of more than one page arrives in pages, and the pool holds each record after the last page.
- A queue of more transactions than the limit goes in more than one `Push`, in order.
- A build that does not know the pages still loads a group.
