# CL-906: Adopt a merge queue once main breaks from merge conflicts

## Goal

Turn on the merge queue only when the data says it is needed.

## Context

In the 2026-10-30 research over 58 repositories (see `.clutch/PLAN.md`), merge queues had no link to throughput at any team size. openclaw merges about 743 PRs a day without one. A queue handles contention between many people merging at once. Brendan decided on 2026-10-30 to keep it out of the auto-merge gate.

## Steps

1. Trigger: `clutch metrics` shows `main` broken by a semantic conflict between two green PRs more than about once a week.
2. Then do CL-402, make `bun run pr merge` queue-aware, decide how stacks enter the queue, and turn the queue on in the `main` ruleset.

## Files

- `.github/workflows/*.yml`
- `tools/github/pr.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- The trigger is recorded with its metric before the queue is turned on.

## Gate

Brendan decides when the trigger holds, and changes the ruleset.
