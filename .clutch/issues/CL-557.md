# CL-557: After three failed fix rounds, the session stops and asks the owner

## Goal

When the same check or blocker still fails after three fix rounds, the session stops and asks the owner. A rerun of a flaky check is recorded, so a person fixes the test later.

## Context

On 2026-11-16 the owner compared Clutch with the article "How the Claude Code team thinks about verification". The owner said to fully meet two practices. A failure needs a person at some point. An agent may not retry a test until it passes.

Today the pages give three numbers. `.agents/skills/README.md` says "after 3 rounds". The babysit skill caps a round at 2 times on one head and 8 rounds a day. The deliver skill and `delivery.md` keep evidence after three attempts, but no page says to ask the owner. A `flaky` rerun leaves no record.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-557-three-rounds-then-owner`.
2. State the rule one time in `docs/contributing/delivery.md`. The deliver and babysit skills point to it.
3. The babysit plan counts the pushed fixes of each failed check. After the third, the plan says that the check needs the owner now, and the round is stuck.
4. A `flaky` rerun is recorded in the `## Babysit log` of the pull request, with the check, the head and the test. The weekly gardening pass reads the logs of the merged pull requests.
5. The "Bounds" section says what each cap stops.

## Acceptance criteria

- A test shows that the plan marks a check as needing the owner now after its third failed fix, and not after the second.
- A test shows that the log item of a `flaky` rerun keeps the test, and that its line says the check was rerun as flaky.
- The numbers that `git grep -n "3 rounds\|three unsuccessful\|at most 8 rounds\|at most 2 times" .agents docs` shows agree with the "Bounds" section.
