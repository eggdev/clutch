# CL-486: Show the friction of the week to the owner for review

## Goal

The owner sees the friction of each week in the weekly digest, and he decides what becomes work.

## Context

An agent records friction with `clutch friction add`, and the entry goes to `.clutch/friction.jsonl`. The weekly gardening pass reads the entries of the week and fixes at most 3 items. Nothing shows the entries to the owner.

Decision record 0006 removed the target of one new check a month from the plan. The owner said on 2026-11-13 that such a target makes agents invent the work. He wants the friction recorded, and he wants a review that prompts him, so that he can judge each entry.

The owner reads the weekly digest each Monday. `.github/workflows/weekly-digest.yml` writes it to the job summary, and `tools/github/owner-findings-digest.ts` adds the owner findings of the week (CL-472).

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-486-friction-digest`.
2. Add a section "Friction" to the weekly digest. It lists each entry of the last 7 days with its kind and its text, and it marks an entry that repeats an earlier one.
3. A repository with no entry gets one line that says so. When the script fails, the section says that the list could not be read, and the digest goes on.
4. Describe the section in `docs/contributing/capabilities/factory-observability.md`, and link it from the weekly gardening section of `docs/contributing/harness-engineering.md`.

## Files

- `.github/workflows/weekly-digest.yml`, and a script next to `tools/github/owner-findings-digest.ts`
- `docs/contributing/capabilities/factory-observability.md`, `docs/contributing/harness-engineering.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- The weekly digest lists each friction entry of the last 7 days, and it marks an entry that repeats.
- A week with no entry prints one line that says so.
- No step sets a target for fixes or for checks.
